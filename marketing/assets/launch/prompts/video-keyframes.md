# Launch video keyframe prompts

Motion generation is intentionally paused. These stills are the approval and shot-lock package.

The approach follows Higgsfield's reference-first Seedance guidance:

- lock the look and object continuity in keyframes before motion;
- state aspect ratio, shot, and duration clearly in the later motion prompt;
- keep early motion tests short;
- describe camera movement and subject movement separately;
- use the minimum references needed for each shot.

## V01 — One More Set

### 01 — Dawn entry

High but human camera position over an ordinary public court at dawn. One fictional adult player enters through the gate with a contemporary graphite racquet and practical court bag. Precise court geometry, long first-light shadows, muted green and terracotta, 28–35 mm perspective, deliberate negative space, restrained grain.

### 02 — Rally

Low professional angle just outside the doubles alley. Two fictional adults are mid-rally with modern graphite racquets. Foreground racquet rim sweeps close to lens; the net cuts diagonally; the far player remains small and alert. Real athletic posture, visible but not artificially frozen ball, hair and fabric motion softness, hard-natural-light shadows.

### 03 — Long lunch

Vertical adaptation of the approved Long Lunch still. Wooden racquets appear here only as stationary table props; they are not used as player equipment.

### 04 — Blue hour

The nearest player stands near the baseline after the last rally, turns back over one shoulder, and loosely holds a modern racquet downward. Floodlights are just beginning to glow. Controlled 50 mm magazine-film feeling, quiet rim light, real fatigue, restrained mixed-light color.

### 05 — Night close

Architectural view from outside the fence. Two small player silhouettes talk near the net under practical floodlights, modern racquets down at their sides. Deep natural darkness, mild lamp halation, muted court color, quiet final mood.

## V04 — After the Last Set

Empty public court at late blue hour. A practical dark canvas bag, two contemporary graphite racquets, cream towel, used balls, and plain metal bottle sit beside a weathered net post. Floodlights are just coming on. Professional object composition, deliberate asymmetry, tactile wear, and no people.

## Library-wide equipment rule

Use contemporary graphite/carbon racquets for players, active court scenes, and modern kit arrangements. A wooden racquet may appear sparingly in a non-playing heritage still life, but it is not the default Racquet Habit prop.
