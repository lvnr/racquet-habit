# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tennis players and style-conscious supporters who see the game as part of
their social life, wardrobe, and daily ritual. They arrive to discover the
brand, understand Collection 01, and buy courtwear, accessories, drinkware, or
collaboration releases without decoding a theatrical commerce interface.

## Product Purpose

Racquet Habit is the ecommerce and editorial home of a contemporary tennis
fashion, objects, and hospitality label based in Yerevan. Success means the
site makes the brand feel credible and ownable, presents products clearly,
converts visitors into customers or Society subscribers, and gives future
suppliers and collaborators a coherent visual standard.

## Brand Personality

Cultivated, sporting, and wry. The emotional goal is the confidence of a
championship programme, the warmth of post-match company, and the quiet humour
of people who said “one more set” two sets ago.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic country-club template, fake aristocratic heritage,
nocturnal black luxury fashion, crypto-adjacent geometry, clinical addiction
role-play, rounded SaaS cards, or an AI-generated editorial moodboard. Avoid
crossed racquets, crowns, laurels, shields, neon, gradients, glassmorphism,
distressed faux-vintage graphics, nonsense lettering, and copied Wimbledon,
Lacoste, Wilson, or Roland-Garros cues.

## Design Principles

1. Let real tennis artifacts and sunlit photography carry the world.
2. Keep commerce plain and legible; reserve wit for supporting copy.
3. Use the approved RH, wordmarks, patch, service arc, and palette as a system.
4. Spend visual boldness on one decisive composition per section.
5. Design for physical credibility: every digital promise should survive print,
   embroidery, packaging, and an eventual owned photoshoot.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, useful
alt text, touch targets of at least 44 px, reduced-motion alternatives, and
layouts that remain usable from 320 px through wide desktop screens. Campaign
casting and language should feel inclusive and social rather than exclusionary
or status-driven.
