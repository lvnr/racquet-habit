# Racquet Habit — New Court Classic

## Positioning

Racquet Habit is the unofficial society for people who always have time for one more set. The identity combines contemporary tournament elegance, tactile sporting graphics, and dry small-print humour.

The system is deliberately sunlit, social, and physical. It must never drift back toward nocturnal tech-luxury, fake aristocracy, or clinical addiction language.

## Typography

- Instrument Serif: editorial display and product names.
- Geist: navigation, body copy, commerce controls.
- Roboto Condensed: collection data and utility labels.
- Yellowtail: temporary implementation lettering for the `Habit` accent only.

The `Habit` script is a production placeholder. Commission a letterer to replace it before large-scale wholesale, permanent packaging, or trademark filing.

## Usage rules

- Keep ordinary layouts to green, ivory, and one accent.
- Use the Society seal at large display scale or as an intentionally patch-like mark.
- Use the monogram for small chest, accessory, and favicon placements.
- Keep the script to a single accent per composition.
- Preserve generous space around every mark.
- Do not add crowns, laurels, shields, crossed racquets, distressing, gradients, chrome, or faux-aged dates.
- Humour belongs in secondary copy. Commerce controls remain plain and useful.

## External production work required

These are not optional if the goal is a genuinely top-tier physical brand:

1. Commission bespoke `Habit` lettering and optical refinement of the full wordmark from a specialist letterer.
2. Have a senior identity designer optically correct the RH monogram and produce final master geometry after real-size print tests.
3. Digitize the monogram and seal for each embroidery substrate; request sew-outs at 12 mm, 25 mm, and 65 mm.
4. Produce spot-colour separations, Pantone/TCX matches, underbase rules, trapping, and screen-print test pulls.
5. Build garment tech packs, graded placement sheets, woven-label specifications, and packaging dielines.
6. Order Fourthwall samples and replace generated mockups with a real product and campaign shoot.
7. Run trademark clearance in the intended apparel, drinkware, retail, and hospitality classes.

The current SVGs and Fourthwall files are production-capable prototypes; these specialist passes are what move them from excellent launch assets to enduring brand masters.
