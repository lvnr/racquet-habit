# Racquet Habit — Launch Marketing & Advertising Plan

**Version 1.0 · July 23, 2026 · Prepared for the social launch of racquethabit.com**

> **V1 superseded July 28, 2026.** Do not execute its culture-first position, launch-grid cadence, or week-three paid ramp. The approved working direction is performance-first: seed six storefront posts and begin Sales/Purchase advertising immediately, as specified in `12-strategy-reset-plan.md`.

Decisions this plan is built on: **global / US-first targeting**, **$1,500–5,000/mo paid budget**, **blended voice** (editorial polish as the base layer; playful clubhouse energy for short-form video and memes).

---

## 1. Where we are

Racquet Habit is live at racquethabit.com with **24 products across five capsules**: Signed Rally — Founding Issue (8), The Daily Lineup (3), Court-Side Pleasures (7), Love Cherries (4), and Repeat Players (2). The current assortment spans tees/crops, one cap, a tote, towel, two drinkware pieces, and a MagSafe case. Fourthwall handles print-on-demand fulfillment and international shipping; checkout settles in USD; Armenia gets free shipping and AMD display pricing. The authoritative production and generation map is `09-current-catalog-production-map.md`.

The brand has something most launch-stage merch brands don't: a **complete, disciplined identity** (New Court Classic system + playful clubhouse direction), a distinctive voice ("the unofficial society for people who always have time for one more set"), and a library of editorial-grade product imagery. Marketing's job is not to invent a brand — it's to put the one that exists in front of the right people, repeatedly, in the formats each platform rewards in 2026.

### The market moment (why now is good)

- **Tenniscore matured from trend to durable aesthetic.** Tennis-skirt search demand cooled (−21% YoY) but the adjacent lifestyle signals — retro trainers +42%, optic white, court-to-café dressing — keep growing. The winning position in 2026 is exactly Racquet Habit's: sell the *culture and humor of racquet life*, not the costume.
- **Pickleball (24.3M US players, +23% YoY) and padel (1M+ US players)** widen the addressable audience for racquet-lifestyle humor. Current designs such as "Emotional Support Racquet," "Out of Office," and "Tennis Is My Rest Day" read cross-sport with little or no copy change.
- **A documented whitespace:** established racquet brands under-serve 18–34s and under-exploit TikTok. A meme-literate, design-led newcomer can own that gap.
- **US Open runway:** Fan Week Aug 23–28, main draw Aug 30–Sep 13. Launching accounts now gives ~4 weeks to seed content and warm ad accounts before the single biggest US tennis-culture moment of the year — then roll straight into Q4 gifting (Pinterest searches start ~45 days before holidays).

## 2. Positioning & audience

### One-line positioning (for every bio, ad account, and pitch)

> Racquet Habit is a tennis society disguised as a merch brand — considered court goods for the hours *around* play. Long lunch. Late match.

### What we are NOT (guardrails from the brand system)

Not performancewear, not fake heritage, not nocturnal luxury, not a generic country-club template. No crossed racquets, crowns, laurels, neon, gradients, distressed faux-vintage. Humor lives in the small print; commerce stays plain and legible.

### Audience segments (in priority order)

| # | Segment | Who | Why they buy | Primary platforms |
|---|---------|-----|--------------|-------------------|
| A | **The Repeat Player** (25–40) | Plays 2–4×/week, club or public courts, tennis is their social calendar | Self-identification — the shirts say what they already feel ("one more set") | IG, TikTok |
| B | **The Court-Adjacent** (22–38) | Tenniscore/style-first; plays casually or not at all; dresses court-to-café | Aesthetic + humor; wears the tee to brunch, not the baseline | IG, Pinterest, TikTok |
| C | **The Gifter** (28–55) | Partner/parent/friend of an obsessed player; searches "tennis gifts" | Gift-ready wit at $40–75; drinkware and caps are entry points | Pinterest, FB, Google |
| D | **Cross-racquet converts** | Pickleball & padel players with the same addiction and disposable income | Same jokes, different court | TikTok, FB Groups |
| E | **Armenia + diaspora** | Yerevan players and the global Armenian community; free local shipping | Local pride — a Yerevan label playing at international level | IG, FB (organic only at launch) |

Segments A+B are the paid-ads core. C activates in Q4 and around tournaments. E is organic warmth and PR, not paid spend, until local volume justifies it.

### Messaging pillars (rotate; never all at once)

1. **The Habit** — obsession as identity. "A difficult habit to break." "One more set." "Previous attempts: inconclusive."
2. **The Long Lunch** — tennis as social ritual. "Court. Lunch. Court." Ice buckets, table settings, something cold.
3. **The Society** — membership feeling without gatekeeping. Founding Issue, small-print certificates, "good people only."
4. **Considered objects** — design quality, garment weight, embroidery detail, the small print itself. (This pillar earns the price point.)

## 3. Channel strategy

### Priority: TikTok + Instagram co-lead · Pinterest as compounding sleeper · Facebook as infrastructure

Your instinct (IG/FB/TikTok/Pinterest) matches the research. Recommended weighting of effort:

| Channel | Role | Effort share | Follower goal (90 days) |
|---------|------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **Instagram** | Brand home; the grid *is* the lookbook; carousels + Reels; where ads convert | 35% | 3–5K |
| **TikTok** | Reach engine + search engine; playful voice lives here; feeds Spark Ads | 30% | 3–10K (volatile) |
| **Pinterest** | High-intent search + gifting; content compounds for months; cheapest clicks | 20% | 500 + 50K monthly views |
| **Facebook** | Ads infrastructure, crossposting, Groups seeding, 35–65 buyer demo | 10% | Page presence only |
| Email (via Fourthwall) | Owns the audience the algorithms rent to us; drop announcements | 5% | 500 subscribers |

**Skip for now:** X (no commerce pull for this category), YouTube (revisit for Shorts syndication in month 2 — free redistribution of TikToks), Snapchat.

### Instagram playbook (2026 rules)

- **Optimize for sends.** Shares/DMs are the #1 ranking signal. Every piece should pass the test: *would one player send this to their doubles partner?* The small-print humor is engineered for exactly this.
- **Format mix:** carousels are the top-performing brand format (0.55% ER; ~2× the views of Reels for small accounts) — use them for drops, design close-ups, meme dumps. Reels at **45–60s** for reach (the measured sweet spot; nothing over 3 min). Stories daily once live.
- **Trial Reels 3–5/week** — shown only to non-followers, invisible on the grid. This is the free A/B lab for hooks in the first 60 days; auto-post winners.
- **Collab posts** with micro-creators and complementary accounts (tennis clubs, run-club-style social tennis accounts) — the highest-leverage new-account distribution tool.
- **Instagram SEO:** "tennis lifestyle brand" and related keywords in the name field ("Racquet Habit | Tennis Lifestyle & Court Goods"), bio, captions, on-screen text, alt text. 3–10 niche hashtags, treated as keywords, not reach magic.
- **Cadence:** 4–5 posts/week (≈2 Reels, 2 carousels, 1 static) + daily Stories + trial reels on top.

### TikTok playbook

- **Cadence: ~1/day for the first 60–90 days** (data: 11+/week = +34% views/post; new accounts need volume for the algorithm to find the audience). Batch-produce weekly; Higgsfield makes this cadence feasible.
- **Voice: full playful clubhouse.** Dry humor delivered deadpan. The account bio sets it: "Tennis Addicts Society. Est. 2026. A difficult habit to break."
- **TikTok SEO:** target long-tail searches — "tennis gifts for her," "what to wear to tennis practice," "pickleball outfit ideas," "tennis aesthetic." Keyword in caption + spoken audio + on-screen text (all indexed).
- **Formats:** POV humor, photo carousels (cheap, high-engagement pattern interrupt), day-in-the-life, before/after, list videos ("5 signs you have a racquet habit"), real sample unboxings, design-process BTS.
- **First 1–3 seconds decide everything.** Hook bank lives in `06-video-ugc-concepts.md`.
- Native tools (CapCut, trending audio ≤ current, Stitch/Duet on tennis moments) boost distribution.

### Pinterest playbook (don't sleep on this one)

- Gen Z is now ~50% of Pinterest; 75% of weekly users are "always shopping"; retail CPCs of $0.50–0.70 are the cheapest of the four platforms. For a giftable, aesthetic-led product at premium prices, Pinterest may quietly become the best ROAS channel by Q4.
- **At launch:** create 8–12 keyword-named boards (exact search phrases): *Tennis Outfit Aesthetic · Tenniscore · Tennis Gifts · Racquet Club Aesthetic · Court Style · Tennis Quotes & Humor · Pickleball Gifts · Tennis Party Ideas · Mediterranean Summer · Clubhouse Interiors.* Pin all product editorials + designs on day one (30–40 pins) so the account looks established immediately.
- **Cadence:** 5–7 fresh pins/week (a "fresh pin" = new image, even for the same product URL — Higgsfield makes infinite fresh variants trivial).
- **Specs:** 2:3 (1000×1500) statics; 6–15s video pins. Titles ≤100 chars with search keywords ("Tennis Gift Idea — Emotional Support Racquet Tee"), natural-language descriptions, **no hashtags** (obsolete there).
- **Claim the website + upload the Fourthwall catalog** → product pins with live prices. Shopping content converts at 2.6× standard pins.
- **Seasonal lead time is 45 days:** US Open content pins should go up NOW (searches build from mid-July); holiday-gift pins by mid-October.

### Facebook playbook

- Complete Page (required for ads; also the social proof layer ad-clickers check). Crosspost IG Reels/carousels natively — zero extra production.
- **Groups are the hidden asset:** tennis/pickleball club and league groups skew 35–65 — a strong buying demo. Participate as Lev (person, not Page) where rules require; share genuinely useful/funny content, never raw promo. 2–3 groups, light touch, ongoing.

## 4. Launch sequence (the first 90 days)

### Phase 0 — Foundation (days 1–7) · *before any public post*

1. Register handles everywhere: `@racquethabit` (fallbacks in `02-social-profiles.md`). Set up profiles from the profile pack: names, bios, links, board structure, highlights.
2. **Ads infrastructure (start immediately — warm-up takes 2 weeks):** Meta Business Manager → verify racquethabit.com domain, business verification, **Pixel + Conversions API** on the storefront, product catalog synced from Fourthwall. TikTok Business + pixel. Pinterest Business + tag + claimed site + catalog. GA4 alongside.
3. Generate the launch asset library with Higgsfield (`08-higgsfield-prompt-pack.md`): ~40 stills, ~12 videos, ~30 pin graphics — enough for 3 weeks of content before you need round two.
4. Seed each platform to "established" state **before follow campaigns begin**: 9–12 IG grid posts (one clean 3×4 grid drop over 2–3 days), 30–40 Pinterest pins across boards, 3–5 TikToks, FB mirror. The launch grid plan is in `03-launch-content-calendar.md`.

### Phase 1 — Organic ignition (weeks 2–5)

- Full cadence: TikTok daily, IG 4–5/wk + 3–5 trial reels/wk, Pinterest 5–7 pins/wk, FB crossposts.
- Meta ad account warm-up: $10–20/day engagement/traffic objectives on best organic content (this builds pixel signal AND page social proof cheaply).
- Recruit 5–10 micro-creators (1–20K followers; tennis players, "tennis girl aesthetic" accounts, pickleball creators). Offer: 1–2 current-catalog pieces + small fee for 1 collab post + content rights (whitelisting for Spark Ads later). Their content becomes both organic collabs and the UGC ad pool.
- Collection-open moment gets a 3-day content arc: tease (silhouettes/details) → launch day (hero film + five-capsule carousel + live product pins) → styling day (how members wear it).
- Weekly ritual content begins (series named in `04-content-topics-and-captions.md`): *Small Print Sunday, The Lineup, Rest Day Diaries, Court Notes.*

### Phase 2 — Paid ramp (weeks 3–8)

- **Week 3:** Meta conversion campaign live at **$50/day**, broad targeting (Andromeda rewards creative diversity over audience hacks), 8–12 genuinely different creatives (mix: 60% UGC-style, 40% editorial/static), 3:2:2 testing structure (3 concepts × 2 formats × 2 hooks). Optimize to Purchase (fall back to Add-to-Cart if volume is too thin the first 2 weeks).
- **Week 4:** Pinterest Performance+ at **$20–30/day** on keyword clusters ("tennis gifts," "tennis outfit," "pickleball gifts," "preppy aesthetic") + shopping ads from the catalog.
- **Week 5–6:** TikTok — **only after organic winners exist** — Spark Ads boosting the top 2–3 proven posts at **$20–50/day**. No cold TikTok conversion campaigns yet.
- Never scale any budget more than +20% per 2–3 days (learning reset). Refresh 3–5 creatives every 1–2 weeks — fashion creative fatigues in ~11–21 days.

### Phase 3 — US Open surge + consolidation (Aug 23 – Sep 13)

- Organic volume up ~50% during Fan Week and the tournament; all reactive/cultural content (see calendar). "The championship you can actually join."
- Shift paid budget toward proven winners; retarget all engagers + site visitors with the drop film and social-proof creative.
- When purchase volume approaches ~50/week, migrate core Meta spend into **Advantage+ Shopping** (10–25% CPA improvement typically follows; cap existing-customer budget at 10–25%).

### Phase 4 — Q4 pivot (from mid-October)

Pinterest gifting content + catalog ads scale first (45-day search lead time), then Meta gift-guide creative from early November. This plan gets its own refresh after launch learnings.

## 5. Paid media plan ($1,500–5,000/mo)

### Budget by month

| Line | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Meta warm-up (engagement/traffic) | $200 | — | — |
| Meta conversions (broad + 3:2:2 testing) | $1,000 (~$50/day from wk 3) | $2,100 (~$70/day) | $2,700–3,600 (ASC, $90–120/day) |
| Pinterest Performance+ & shopping | $300 ($20/day from wk 4) | $600 ($20/day) | $750 ($25/day) |
| TikTok Spark Ads | — | $600 ($20/day) | $900 ($30/day) |
| Creator seeding/fees (not ad spend but same wallet) | ~$300–500 | ~$500 | ~$500 |
| **Total ad spend** | **~$1,500** | **~$3,300** | **~$4,400–5,250** |

### Benchmarks to plan against (2026 apparel/DTC)

- Meta: apparel CPC ~$0.89, CPA ~$22, CVR ~3.2%, ROAS ~4× at maturity — expect *worse* for the first 4–6 weeks while the pixel learns; treat month 1 as data acquisition.
- TikTok: CPM $4–13, ecommerce CPA ~$33 median.
- Pinterest: CPC $0.50–0.70 retail, CPMs 30–50% below Meta.
- With a ~$65 AOV (tee + something), break-even ROAS is roughly 1.6–1.9× depending on Fourthwall margins — compute the real number from your per-product margin before scaling anything.

### Creative doctrine (this decides success more than budgets)

1. **Creative is the targeting.** Meta's Andromeda matches ads to people by reading the creative itself. Go broad; differentiate with 8–12 *conceptually distinct* ads (a meme ad, a UGC try-on, an editorial still, and real object-level ASMR are four concepts; four color variants of one still are one concept).
2. **60/40 UGC-to-polished for prospecting; invert for retargeting.** UGC-style creative runs +27% CTR / +19% CVR for DTC.
3. **Hook in <2s, brand/product in <3s, 15–30s total** for direct-response video; 4:5 for feed statics (it has replaced 1:1), 9:16 for everything vertical; respect the unified Meta safe zones (top 14%, bottom 20–35%, sides 6%).
4. **AI disclosure:** Meta requires self-certification for photorealistic AI people; TikTok requires the AIGC toggle; Pinterest auto-labels. Use the toggles — labeled content suffers minimal penalty, unlabeled content gets throttled or the account penalized. Full details + which creatives need it flagged in `07-ads-creative-pack.md`.
5. Kill/refresh rules: CTR −10% WoW, CPA +15% WoW, or frequency >3.0 → rotate the creative out.

### Measurement

- Source of truth: Fourthwall orders + GA4 (UTM every link; link-in-bio tool with per-platform UTMs).
- Weekly scorecard (15 min): spend, CPM, CTR, CPA, ROAS per platform; top/bottom creative; follower + email deltas. Template in `07-ads-creative-pack.md §9`.
- North-star for the first 90 days: **cost per first purchase ≤ $30** blended, **500+ email subscribers**, **one organic format that repeatably clears 10K views** — that format becomes the scaling backbone.

## 7. Risks & honest notes

- **Premium POD pricing vs. impulse-buy merch:** the content must sell *brand and belonging*, not commodity tees — that's why the Society framing leads everywhere. Watch AOV; accessories and drinkware are the ad-friendly entry points. Use live storefront prices in creative rather than fixed price claims in this plan.
- **AI-generated content at scale:** the editorial assets are excellent, but platforms and users increasingly detect and discount obvious AI. Mitigation: blend in real-world content ASAP (creator UGC from Phase 1, real product photos of actual Fourthwall samples, Yerevan court photos), use AI disclosure toggles, and never let generated imagery promise a physical detail the product doesn't have (already a brand rule — it matters double in ads).
- **Fourthwall production/shipping times and packaging** on POD: set expectations in FAQ highlights and ad comments ("made to order — allow X days") to protect review scores. Default outer packaging varies by fulfillment partner and cannot be treated as a branded prop. Do not publish an unboxing concept until a real sample order is photographed; a supported 6×4 or 4×6 custom insert is the only standard POD branding layer available.
- **One-person content operation:** the cadence above is aggressive. The Higgsfield prompt pack is engineered for batching (one session = a week of content). If it still overloads, cut FB to pure crossposting and drop TikTok to 5×/week before touching IG/Pinterest.
- **Trademark/IP:** avoid tournament names/marks in paid creative ("US Open" as cultural commentary in organic is normal; in ad copy it invites trouble — use "the Open," "tennis's biggest fortnight").
