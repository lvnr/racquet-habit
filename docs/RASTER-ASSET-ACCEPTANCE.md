# Racquet Habit — raster asset acceptance checklist

Use this checklist after every individual ChatGPT generation. Do not submit the
next variant until the current image is saved or explicitly rejected.

## Universal hard rejects

- Any third-party logo, recognizable tournament branding, sponsor block, or
  generated signage.
- Misspelled or nonsense text. Production brand typography is added from the
  approved SVG system after generation.
- Malformed hands, duplicated limbs, fused anatomy, impossible object contact,
  or a racquet with warped head, strings, grip, throat, or perspective.
- Floating objects, contradictory shadows, implausible closures or packaging
  structures, duplicated products, or materially impossible surfaces.
- Generic black luxury mood, teal/orange cinema grading, fake fog, glossy CGI,
  fake sepia nostalgia, heavy distress, or shallow focus that hides the scene.
- A composition that could be relabelled for any generic tennis brand without
  losing meaning.

## Campaign hero

- Correct 3:2 landscape composition at the highest available resolution.
- The specified copy-safe zone remains genuinely quiet at desktop and 4:5 crop.
- Subject, racquet, clothing, court and sunlight all read as one real moment.
- Whites retain fabric detail; green stays close to Centre Court Green rather
  than cyan, black or fluorescent.
- Warm, cultivated, inclusive, social energy—not aggressive sports advertising.

## Court still life

- Exact object count and no unrequested props.
- Racquet and cap construction are credible at product-detail zoom.
- Contact shadows, condensation, felt, cotton and paper look physically real.
- Composition has a usable crop and a controlled negative-space zone.

## Tournament poster

- No baked-in lettering or logo.
- Spot-colour logic survives conversion to four inks.
- Required top and bottom type-safe zones are truly empty.
- Halftone and misregistration are selective, not a blanket nostalgia filter.
- Geometry is original, balanced and vector-reconstructable.

## Packaging suite

- All eight specified components appear exactly once.
- Structures can be manufactured; folds, cords, closures and thickness agree.
- Identity zones remain blank enough for approved vector overlay.
- The system reads as contemporary tennis merchandise, not cosmetics or wine.

## Export and record

- Download the original, not a screenshot.
- Record thread, prompt variant, date, model, pixel dimensions, decision and
  rejection reason in `docs/ASSET-REVIEW-LOG.md`.
- Preserve the untouched download under `design/brand-v3/concepts/`.
- Derivatives and web crops belong under `public/images/v3/`; never overwrite
  the untouched source.
