# Racquet Habit v3 brand assets

This directory contains the production candidate identity system developed from
the founder-approved ChatGPT image references.

## Approved RH monogram

- `logo-rh-monogram.svg` — Centre Court Green master
- `logo-rh-monogram-ivory.svg` — reverse-use master
- `logo-rh-monogram-black.svg` — one-colour production master
- `logo-rh-monogram-embroidery.svg` — strengthened small embroidery master
- `mark-rh-icon.svg` — square digital icon
- `icons/` — favicon and app/touch-icon exports
- `exports/` — transparent PNG exports at 1x, 2x, and 4x
- `design/brand-v3/proofs/rh-monogram-production-proof.png` — master/vector/size comparison

The exact approved raster is
`design/brand-v3/concepts/02-rh-monogram-approved-master.png`. Do not substitute the earlier
geometric RH prototype.

## Colour usage

- Centre Court Green: `#103C2C`
- Championship Ivory: `#F3EBD8`
- Black production proof: `#000000`

Use green on ivory or ivory on green for normal brand applications. Use the
black file only for supplier artwork, separations, and single-ink proofing.

## Minimum size

- Standard print: 12 mm wide
- Embroidery: 15 mm wide, using the embroidery master
- Digital icon: verified at 16 px and 32 px

Physical embroidery sampling remains required before bulk production.

## Selected wordmark system

- `logo-primary-editorial.svg` — stacked Direction C primary lockup
- `logo-primary-horizontal.svg` — Direction A navigation and label lockup
- matching `-ivory` and `-black` one-colour variants
- transparent PNG exports at 1x, 2x, and 4x in `exports/`

The raster-to-vector comparison is
`design/brand-v3/wordmark-selected/wordmark-vector-proof.png`. The selected
wordmarks survive the 160 px digital test. Keep the horizontal lockup at least
160 px wide and the editorial lockup at least 120 px wide in ordinary digital
use. A specialist optical-lettering pass remains recommended before large
physical production.

## Society patch

- `logo-society-patch.svg` — green, ivory, and Royal Trim production candidate
- `logo-society-patch-one-colour.svg` — green/ivory woven variant
- `logo-society-patch-black-proof.svg` — separation and supplier proof

The patch is designed for a 68 mm woven application. Its 38 mm proof is a
reduced-use test; prefer the 68 mm master on caps and bags. A physical weave
sample is still required before bulk production.

## Service arc and bounce mark

- `logo-racquet-flourish.svg` — 120 mm print-scale service arc
- `mark-bounce.svg` — exactly eight open-centre impact rays
- matching `-ivory` and `-black` variants

The service arc keeps separate approach/exit strokes, a solid impact point, and
a deliberate post-impact gap. Use the bounce mark as a micro accent rather than
as a repeated decorative star field.

## Collection 01 production artwork

Eight coordinated, fully outlined SVG masters and matching 300 ppi PNG exports
live in `public/merch-v3/`. They cover two apparel back prints, a court towel,
Society tote, two drinkware wraps, and two hospitality collaboration labels.
The visual review board is
`design/brand-v3/merch-selected/collection-01-production-artwork-proof.png`.

These are production artwork masters, not supplier dielines. Fit them to the
exact Fourthwall or manufacturer print-area template and approve a physical
sample before sale. Coffee and seasonal-drink labels also require final net
weight, ingredient, origin, allergen, nutrition, barcode, and local compliance
copy from the selected collaborator.
