# IG05 — Out of Office — Professional Alternates

These alternates retain the story and exact cap from the authentic frames while applying the user's professional editorial references as visual direction only.

## Direction

- stronger court and net geometry
- more deliberate negative space
- controlled hard-natural-light shadow design
- professional on-location fashion composition
- cleaner garment presentation
- real pores, flyaway hair, cotton wrinkles, worn paint, and restrained film grain

Do not copy people, wardrobe, logos, text, or exact scenes from the art references.

## Racquet correction

Both player frames use a contemporary graphite/carbon tennis racquet with plausible modern frame geometry, strings, and grip. Wooden racquets are not used in active-court or player imagery.
