## Photography decision

Two compatible lanes are represented:

- **Authentic/observational:** candid, practical light, imperfect framing, and tactile grain.
- **Professional editorial:** stronger geometry, controlled hard-natural-light shadows, cleaner garment presentation, and premium magazine framing without synthetic retouching.

The direct comparisons are:

- `instagram/IG05-out-of-office/`
- `instagram/IG10-tennis-lunch-tennis/`

The professional Out of Office pair also corrects the earlier wooden-racquet repetition. Modern graphite/carbon racquets are now the default for players and active-court imagery.

## Video approval gate

After still approval:

1. write shot-specific Seedance prompts with aspect ratio, duration, camera move, and subject action stated explicitly;
2. run short draft motion tests;
3. review continuity, anatomy, product stability, and motion;
4. render standard/final motion only for locked shots;
5. assemble the clean master, Instagram edit, TikTok edit, cover, SRT, sound note, and disclosure note.
