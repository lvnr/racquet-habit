## Frame 01 — Friends at the table

Two fictional adult friends pause between lunch and tennis at an ordinary courtside café table. One wears the washed navy oversized tee; the other wears the washed dark-brown crop. They are caught mid-laugh and mid-reach rather than posing. Lunch remnants, an ordinary glass, a striped napkin, used tennis balls, and racquets create believable clutter. Practical daylight, off-center framing, real skin and cotton, restrained color, slight motion softness, and 35 mm grain.

## Frame 02 — Navy tee

The navy-tee subject sits sideways in a simple metal café chair outside the court, laughing toward her friend while holding a small used plate. The complete garment artwork remains readable. A racquet, ball, scuffed table, and plain glass ground the scene.

## Frame 03A — Brown crop, authentic lane

The brown-crop subject stands at the open public-court gate, gathering a racquet and striped towel while looking back toward her friend. Natural late-afternoon light, imperfect crop, ordinary court wear, and restrained grain keep the frame observational.

## Frame 03B — Brown crop, professional lane

Create a professionally art-directed version of frame 03A. Keep the same story and exact garment, but use cleaner editorial geometry, deliberate negative space, a more intentional camera angle, hard-natural-light shadow design, controlled skin tones, crisp garment presentation, and premium magazine framing. Retain pores, flyaway hair, cotton wrinkles, worn paint, and subtle grain so it remains photographic rather than glossy or synthetic.

Use the user-supplied professional references for visual direction only. Do not reproduce their people, wardrobe, logos, typography, or exact scenes.

Replace the wooden racquet with a contemporary graphite/carbon racquet. Modern racquets are required for player and active-court imagery throughout the library; wooden racquets are reserved for occasional non-playing heritage still lifes.
