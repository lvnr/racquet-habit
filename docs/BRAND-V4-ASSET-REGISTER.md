1. **Primary signature** — `public/brand-v4/logo-primary-organic.svg`
   - Restores the organic handwritten `Habit`.
   - Use on campaign panels, packaging fronts, large garment graphics, and collection title cards.

2. **Horizontal navigation signature**
   - `public/brand-v4/logo-wordmark-organic-horizontal.svg`

   - Use in navigation, invoices, labels, and narrow spaces.

3. **Formal logomark** — `public/brand-v4/logo-rh-curving.svg`
   - The founder-selected curving serif RH.
   - Use for favicon, embroidery, cap front, racquet accessories, product stamps, and formal collection markers.

4. **Campaign symbol** — `public/brand-v4/mark-court-signature.svg`
   - A broad asymmetric court/ball gesture.
   - Use only at large editorial sizes until its small-scale silhouette receives a final specialist optical pass.

5. **Secondary gesture**
   - `public/brand-v4/mark-rally-loop.svg`
   - `public/brand-v4/graphics/rally-arc.svg`
   - Use as a trajectory, crop, or interruption—not as another logo.
