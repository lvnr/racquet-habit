# Racquet Habit — external production brief

The digital New Court Classic identity, launch imagery, storefront, and Fourthwall product files are complete. The remaining work is specialist physical-production craft, not another round of generic visual exploration.

## Commission before a major commercial launch

### Lettering and identity masters

- Bespoke `Habit` lettering to replace the temporary implementation script.
- Optical correction of the RH monogram and wordmark at display, garment, and 12–20 mm sizes.
- Final AI, SVG, EPS, and press-ready PDF masters with spacing and misuse rules.
- Trademark clearance for the name, monogram, motto, and relevant product classes.

### Embroidery and print engineering

- Substrate-specific embroidery digitization and physical sew-outs at 12 mm, 25 mm, and 65 mm.
- Pantone/TCX matches, spot-colour separations, underbase, trapping, and screen-print strike-offs.
- Woven-patch, woven-label, heat-transfer, foil, deboss, and one-colour production variants.

### Product development

- Fit blocks and graded tech packs for original tee, polo, sweater, cap, and warm-up layer.
- Fabric, wash, shrinkage, colourfastness, abrasion, movement, and sweat testing.
- Custom towel, racquet tote, dampener, grip tape, and drinkware development with compliance documentation.
- Fourthwall samples before approving the demo capsule for paid promotion.

### Packaging

- Manufacturer-ready dielines for mailer, hangtag, membership card, tissue, labels, inserts, and fragile-product protection.
- Real substrate, ink, foil, transit, scuff, moisture, and drop tests.

### Photography

- Replace generated campaign art with an owned shoot once physical samples exist.
- Sunlit court and quiet clubhouse setups; on-body, movement, studio, macro, and all required social crops.
- Usage rights for web, organic and paid social, retail, press, and out-of-home.

### Commerce, legal, and accessibility

- Final terms, privacy, returns, warranty, duties, tax, and food/beverage compliance review.
- Independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit and moderated checkout tests in Armenia, the US, and an EU market.
- Test refunds, replacements, lost parcels, damaged goods, and address corrections end to end.

See [docs/BRAND-SYSTEM.md](./docs/BRAND-SYSTEM.md) for the approved identity system and [docs/ASSET-PROMPTS.md](./docs/ASSET-PROMPTS.md) for the image-generation prompts.
