# First-Pass Social Generation Plan

**Working production brief · July 28, 2026**

> **Deprecated July 28, 2026.** Do not continue generating this library. It is retained as a record of V1; the replacement is a performance-first set of 12 still treatments, followed by eight sales videos after still approval. See `12-strategy-reset-plan.md`.

This is the first bounded content batch for turning the new Racquet Habit profiles into convincing, active brand accounts. It covers the launch seed grid and enough native content for Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, Stories, and Highlights without trying to manufacture the entire 30-day calendar at once.

The goal is not maximum volume. It is a coherent first impression:

1. **Brand world:** editorial tennis, lunch, wit, late light.
2. **Product truth:** the actual 24-product catalog, accurately shown.
3. **Club language:** memorable lines and repeatable content franchises.
4. **Movement:** enough video that the profiles do not look like a static lookbook.

## 1. Locked inputs and production rules

### Authoritative inputs

- Product truth: `design/brand-fixed/production/<product>/catalog/`
- Product-specific mood only: `design/brand-fixed/production/<product>/editorial-court-archive/`
- Identity only: `design/brand-fixed/identity/`
- Carefully selected secondary brand material: `design/brand-fixed/assets/`
- Current catalog and Higgsfield Element map: `09-current-catalog-production-map.md`

`catalog/` establishes the product. Court Archive images establish atmosphere, framing, light, and styling; they are never the product reference.

### Locked visual system

- Forest: `#24493A`
- Ivory: `#FFF7EE`
- Signal red: `#E93622`
- Display: Instrument Serif
- Sans: Geist
- Condensed utility copy: Roboto Condensed
- Generated typography is never final typography. Headlines, captions, labels, logos, and end cards are assembled deterministically with the real identity assets.

### Photography lanes

- **Observational:** candid, practical light, imperfect framing, tactile 35 mm texture, and real court wear.
- **Professional editorial:** deliberate geometry and negative space, controlled hard-natural-light shadow design, cleaner garment presentation, and commissioned magazine framing.

Both lanes retain real skin and surface texture. Neither uses waxy retouching, glossy CGI, generic luxury-resort styling, or uncanny hyperrealism. User-supplied professional photography references and cinematic stills are used only for visual and art direction—not their people, wardrobe, logos, typography, or exact scenes.

### Racquet rule

Use contemporary graphite/carbon racquets in player, active-court, and modern kit imagery. Wooden racquets may appear sparingly in non-playing heritage still lifes or stationary table arrangements; they are not the recurring default prop.

### Truth and disclosure

- Never invent product variants, prints, embroidery, packaging, materials, pricing, or fulfillment details.
- Do not make fit, feel, durability, delivery, or first-person purchase claims until physical samples or real creators can substantiate them.
- No synthetic founder story and no fictional unboxing.
- Photorealistic AI people are disclosed using the platform's AI-content control where available.
- Marc is an AI club member, not a customer. Ani is a real cofounder; do not synthesize her voice or personal testimony without a separate approval.

## 5. Four-video launch pack

Every video receives:

- one clean 9:16 master without platform UI;
- one Instagram edit;
- one TikTok edit;
- an SRT caption file even when speech is absent;
- one cover frame with real typography;
- a music/sound note and disclosure note.

### V1 — “One More Set” launch film

- **Length:** 15–20 seconds for the first release.
- **Structure:** sunrise court → rally → Long Lunch → glance back at court → dusk/night return.
- **Type beats:** “Long lunch.” / “Late match.” / “One more set.” / wordmark.
- **Build:** 4–5 approved keyframes; Seedance 2.0 Mini for motion tests; Standard only after shot lock.
- **Audio:** original sound design or licensed/platform-native audio, with a clean audio-free master retained.
- **Products:** only where an exact reference can remain stable. The film can succeed as a brand-world piece without forcing a product into every shot.

### V4 — “POV: one more set”

- **Length:** 10–12 seconds.
- **Visual:** bag and racquets left beside an empty dusk court, then floodlights switch on or players re-enter frame.
- **Overlay:** “POV: you said ‘last one’ three sets ago.”
- **Build:** one locked hero keyframe; minimal motion; the joke carries the asset.
- **Purpose:** native, low-friction shareability.

### V11 — “What’s in the court bag”

- **Length:** 15–20 seconds.
- **Objects:** racquet, balls, Something Cold tote, Out of Office cap, Serve Chilled tumbler, one current tee, cherries.
- **Structure:** top-down sequential bag dump with one dry label per object.
- **Build:** exact catalog/product Elements; no character required. Use Nano Banana Pro only for the locked top-down arrangement, then Seedance or an edit-driven reveal.
- **Truth rule:** it is a styled brand inventory, not a customer testimonial.

### V12 — Court-side hydration

- **Length:** 12–15 seconds.
- **Objects:** Court-Side Hydration travel mug and Serve Chilled tumbler.
- **Structure:** match-score cadence follows a pour; pause; “one more set.”
- **Build:** product-faithful stills → restrained motion. Prioritize liquid, ice, condensation, and sound design without distorting the vessels.

## 9. Generation routing and credit discipline

|---|---|---|
| Product cards, type cards, carousels, covers | Deterministic composition using real files | Manual design refinement |
| Single-product editorial still | Native GPT Image 2 with catalog reference | Nano Banana Pro if product correction is needed |
| Multi-product editorial still | Native draft only if references remain exact | Nano Banana Pro with current Higgsfield Elements |

| Motion test | Seedance 2.0 Mini | Seedance 2.0 Standard after shot approval |
| Recurring Ani/Marc imagery | Do not put on critical path while Souls are queued | Soul 2.0 after one canonical identity is ready |

Recraft is not needed for this launch batch. Marketing Studio can be reserved for the later DTC-ad batch once the organic visual system is approved.

## 13. Final QA checklist

An asset is publishable only when:

- the named product matches the current catalog;
- front/back artwork and color are not invented or mirrored;
- no old Fourthwall mockup was used;
- product edges, hands, racquets, balls, lids, handles, and court lines are physically plausible;
- logos and type come from real identity assets;
- copy is readable at phone size;
- a Reel/TikTok still makes sense without sound;
- all videos have a clean master and captions;
- platform crop and UI safe areas are checked;
- AI disclosure is noted in the publishing manifest;
- the destination URL is correct;
- no unsupported pricing, fit, material, shipping, packaging, or customer claim appears.

## 14. Explicitly outside this first pass

- Founder-story film with synthetic Ani/Lev voice or testimony.
- Unboxing, mailers, tissue, inserts, or packaging visuals before a real Fourthwall sample.
- AI “customer review” content.
- Ani/Marc recurring-character series before a canonical Soul is ready and reviewed.
- Paid-ad variations, offer cards, retargeting testimonials, and creator whitelisting.
- The full week 1–4 calendar. Those assets should be chosen using real performance data from this seed batch.

The immediate next production unit is **Round A**. It is small enough to review as a system and representative enough to prevent expensive inconsistency later.
