# Photoshoot & Image Concepts (P1–P12)

Editorial concepts for stills — each with purpose, composition, styling, and platform fit. All are generated in Higgsfield first (prompts mapped 1:1 in file 08 §C), then progressively replaced/augmented with real photography as samples and creators come online. Every concept obeys the brand photography rules: sunlit, tactile, social, real skin and fabric texture, unusual angles welcome; no cinematic teal, no lonely-luxury stare, no generic club stock.

**Global look:** Mediterranean court world — clay and worn hard courts, warm stone, cypress, striped canvas, dry summer light or floodlit night. Identity palette anchored to Court Green `#24493A`, Ivory `#FFF7EE`, and Signal Red `#E93622`; product-specific colors come only from that product's current catalog references. Casting: diverse, 20s–30s, social energy, mid-laughter or mid-motion, never posed-luxury.

---

**P1 — The Long Lunch (hero campaign).** A linen-covered table set directly on the baseline: carafe, citrus, ice bucket of tennis balls and the exact Serve Chilled tumbler, two racquets resting like cutlery. Two or three members mid-conversation, one wearing the exact Signed Rally Founding Issue oversized tee and one still holding a racquet. Overhead sun, hard shadows. Wide hero + overhead tablescape detail. *Use: IG hero, FB cover, Pinterest board cover, site sync.*

**P2 — Tennis Lunch Tennis.** The Midnight tee and Brown crop styled at a café table courtside; a schedule card ("12:00 LUNCH / 1:30 TENNIS") propped against a glass; fork and ball on the same plate. Front/back pair shot. *Use: product editorial, ad static.*

**P3 — Rest Day.** Member horizontal on a striped lounger, racquet held on chest like a book; court net visible behind. Exact Tennis Is My Rest Day tee from the current catalog reference. Second frame: verified print close-up. *Use: carousel, meme-adjacent editorial.*

**P4 — Out of Office.** A briefcase and laptop abandoned at the net post, jacket draped over the net cord; member walking away toward the baseline wearing the exact Out of Office Court Cap, not looking back. Pair with a plain unbranded top—there is no current Out of Office tee. Late-afternoon light, long shadows. *Use: editorial + ad concept "Return time unconfirmed."*

**P5 — Night Court (reverse fixture).** Floodlit hard court at night, moths in the light beams, deep green/burgundy tees, high-contrast flash-style shots against chain-link. The Night Court Reverse tee's natural habitat. *Use: grid variety (dark row), drop tease.*

**P6 — Serve Chilled (still life).** Museum-grade still life: the exact Serve Chilled tumbler beside a galvanized ice bucket, tennis balls buried in ice like champagne, lemon wheel, condensation; ivory sweep background, single hard light. *Use: The Lineup series, Pinterest, ad static.*

**P7 — Court-Side Pleasures (flat-lay).** Beach towel spread on clay, tote spilling: the current cap, Serve Chilled tumbler, balls, cherries, paperback, and sunscreen. Directly overhead. Alternate: same kit on a clubhouse bench. *Use: gift-angle carousel, Pinterest (2:3 crops beautifully).*

**P8 — The Locker Room.** Ivory lockers, five open compartments representing the five capsules. Use a tightly curated current assortment: Signed Rally tee, Tennis Lunch crop, Love Cherries case, Out of Office cap, Serve Chilled tumbler. Numbered like the site's product cards. *Use: collection tease + launch carousel.*

**P9 — Something Cold (departure).** The exact Something Cold Organic Court Tote beside a weekender and racquet bag at a sun-washed doorway or vintage car trunk; towel edge and tennis balls visible, with no invented product graphics. *Use: editorial, travel-season content.*

**P10 — Love Cherries (pink court).** Blush/pink backdrop or rose-tinted clay; cherries-made-of-tennis-balls styled in a glass bowl; crop tee + oversized tee pairing; playful, slightly surreal. *Use: Love Cherries collection refresh, Valentine's bank.*

**P11 — Small Print (macro series).** Extreme close-ups of details that visibly exist in current references: Society/RH monogram embroidery, Signed Rally and Love Cherries print texture, cap stitching, vessel artwork, case surface, and cotton weave. Shallow depth, tactile. *Use: Small Print Sunday, trust-building for premium pricing.*

**P12 — Yerevan Home Court.** Warm stone architecture, local court with mountain light, members walking with racquets past tuff-stone walls. The origin story made visible — global brand, real place. *Use: founder note, About highlight, PR.*

## Coverage checklist per concept (when generating or shooting)

1 wide hero (4:5 + 16:9 crop) · 1 detail/macro · 1 front/back product pair · 1 candid in-motion frame · 1 vertical 9:16 for Stories/pins. Generate at the highest quality tier, upscale to 4K for anything going to ads or Pinterest.

## Real-world shoot note (month 2+)

When Fourthwall samples arrive: replicate P1, P6, P11 with real product first (the ice bucket, the tablescape, and macros are cheap to art-direct at home and instantly de-AI the feed). Then a half-day golden-hour shoot with 2–4 friends at a local Yerevan court covers P2/P4/P12 with authentic frames the algorithms and users increasingly reward.
