# Racquet Habit — external production brief

Most brand concepting, digital artwork, campaign previsualization, and website
design can be completed in-house with the current ChatGPT + vector workflow.
The following work should still be commissioned to specialists for a genuinely
top-tier launch.

## 1. Final wordmark lettering

Commission a lettering designer after the preferred generated wordmark is
selected. Deliverables:

- optically drawn `RACQUET HABIT` primary and horizontal lockups;
- custom `Habit` lettering rather than a licensed script used unchanged;
- corrected Q, R, H, joins, terminals, spacing, and small-size alternates;
- one-colour SVG, EPS, PDF, and outlined source files;
- green, ivory, and black production masters;
- minimum-size and clear-space specifications.

The approved RH monogram is already reconstructed as a clean vector. The
letterer should harmonize the wordmark with it, not redesign the monogram.

## 2. Embroidery digitizing and physical sew-out

Hire the cap/garment supplier's experienced digitizer. Request:

- 15 mm and 22 mm RH monogram sew-outs;
- tatami/satin stitch comparison;
- pull compensation and underlay adjusted for twill and knit separately;
- thread matches for Centre Court Green, Championship Ivory, and Royal Trim;
- photograph and approve a real sample before production.

An embroidery-safe vector is supplied, but digitizing is a manufacturing skill,
not a generic SVG export.

## 3. Packaging and garment production files

Once suppliers are selected, commission final dielines and tech packs for:

- woven cap patch, neck label, care label, and hem label;
- hangtag, membership card, shipping insert, box, and tissue repeat;
- coffee pouch, roast label, cup sleeve, bottle wrap, and food-safe labels;
- towel jacquard or sublimation repeat;
- tote pocket/piping construction and placement specification.

Generated packaging boards are art-direction references only. Production art
must use the manufacturer's exact bleed, safe area, ink, material, and finishing
requirements.

## 4. Owned campaign and product photography

AI campaign imagery is suitable for launch previsualization and initial website
art direction. Replace it with a real shoot when physical samples exist:

- one half-day sunlit court shoot with male and female talent;
- one controlled product/still-life setup;
- full product coverage on neutral background for commerce accuracy;
- landscape, 4:5, square, and 9:16 compositions captured intentionally;
- colour-managed files and retouching that preserves fabric texture.

Do not sell a specific product using an AI image that misrepresents garment cut,
material, print placement, or hardware.

## 5. Licensing and legal

- License the final commercial display and UI fonts for web, print, and apparel.
- Run professional trademark clearance for `RACQUET HABIT` and the RH device in
  the launch markets and relevant apparel/retail classes.
- Have food and beverage partners approve ingredients, allergens, claims, and
  required local label language.

These are the areas where specialist judgment and physical proofing materially
change quality or risk. They should not be replaced by extra AI generations.
