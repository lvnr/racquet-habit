## Raster selection decision

The complete comparison board is
`design/brand-v3/review/raster-asset-selection-01.png`.

- Hero C has the strongest owned architectural composition and cleanest copy
  zone. Hero A is retained because its closer crop reads better on mobile.
- Still life A is the most disciplined and commerce-compatible. B is retained
  for a more lived-in editorial story.
- Poster A is the clearest high-impact Collection 01 asset. C is retained for a
  future expressive garment or print.
- Packaging A communicates the complete system without drifting toward
  warehouse utility or hospitality/cosmetics styling.

## Review criteria

- Distinctive at a glance without needing tennis clip art.
- Coherent with the complete New Court Classic system.
- Clear hierarchy at the actual intended size.
- Physically plausible for its material and process.
- No malformed equipment, anatomy, labels, or typography.
- No accidental third-party resemblance.
- No decorative detail that cannot survive production.
- Strong enough in one colour before accent colour is added.

## Founder decisions

### 01 — primary wordmark

The earlier downloaded lockup is not approved. Although its Roman `RACQUET`
has useful tournament character, `Habit` is still a generic brush script with
an unnecessary long underline. It also behaves like a second logo pasted below
the Roman rather than a coherent wordmark family.

The reset comparison solved that mismatch. The selected system is:

- Direction C for the primary editorial lockup: a compact stacked Roman/italic
  composition with a short finish and clear hierarchy.
- Direction A for horizontal navigation, labels, and narrow packaging.

Both were reconstructed as one-colour paths and verified against their raster
reference at 160 px. They are production-ready digital candidates; a specialist
letterer should still perform the final optical pass before high-volume physical
production.

### 02 — RH monogram

Approved reference: `design/brand-v3/references/rh-monogram-selected-reference.png`

Downloaded high-resolution original:
`design/brand-v3/concepts/02-rh-monogram-approved-master.png`

Production vectors:

- `public/brand-v3/logo-rh-monogram-black.svg`
- `public/brand-v3/logo-rh-monogram-embroidery.svg`

- Keep the large, sculptural high-contrast R.
- Keep the separate H with the R's diagonal leg acting as the visual cross-stroke.
- Keep the confident italic posture and the fine upward terminal on the right.
- Do not replace it with the earlier blocky, geometric, or sans-serif prototypes.
- Production cleanup may regularize curves, joins, and minimum embroidery widths,
  but must not lose the approved gesture.

### 05 — championship pattern system

but its fourth tile reverted to literal tennis-ball circles. The production
system keeps the stronger abstract seam-arc interpretation.
