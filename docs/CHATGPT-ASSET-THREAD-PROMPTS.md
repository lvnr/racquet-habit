# Racquet Habit — ChatGPT asset thread prompts

Use each numbered section as the opening message of a separate chat inside the
`Racquet Habit — Brand Studio` ChatGPT project. The project sources and project
instructions supply the shared brand context.

Each first pass is intentionally a single three-panel comparison sheet. This
makes the art direction comparable before spending generations on isolated
production masters. After choosing a direction, regenerate that direction by
itself at the requested aspect ratio and highest available quality.

## 01 — Primary wordmark and Habit lettering

```text
THREAD 01 — PRIMARY WORDMARK & HABIT LETTERING

Use image generation now. Create one landscape 3:2 professional lettering
exploration sheet on a perfectly flat Championship Ivory background. Show three
large, clearly separated, one-colour Centre Court Green wordmark directions,
labelled only A, B, and C in tiny neutral sans type. No mockups, shadows,
objects, textures, or explanatory paragraphs inside the image.

All three must spell exactly RACQUET HABIT and be meaningfully different:

A — TOURNAMENT SWEEP
RACQUET in a confident high-contrast uppercase Roman with enough body for
screen print. Habit is bespoke fast brush-italic lettering crossing the lower
third, with one compact finishing stroke that implies movement. Elegant but
athletic; never wedding calligraphy.

B — EDITORIAL SIGNATURE
A more restrained horizontal masthead. RACQUET has slightly narrower Roman
proportions and custom Q construction. Habit behaves like a decisive autograph
under the final five letters, with less overlap and excellent navigation-size
legibility.

C — CENTRE-MARK LOCKUP
A stacked RACQUET / HABIT composition. The Roman and italic share one optical
axis and one purposeful cross-stroke, creating a recognizable silhouette for a
garment back or tournament programme cover without becoming a badge.

Judge the designs as real one-colour marks at 35 mm print width and 160 px web
width. Avoid generic Didot typing, fragile hairlines, swashy script, racquet or
ball clip art, third-party references, and added copy. Generate the comparison
image rather than merely describing it.
```
