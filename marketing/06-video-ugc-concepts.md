# Video & UGC Concepts (V1–V24) + Hook Bank

Formats sized for 2026: 15–30s TikTok/DR ads, 45–60s IG Reels, 6–15s Pinterest video pins. Sound-on design, captions always. Higgsfield prompt mappings in file 08 §D–E.

## §1 Brand films (polished layer)

**V1 — "One More Set" brand film (45–60s IG / 30s cut).** Montage: sunrise court → serve → net exchange → racquets down → long lunch → glances at the court → back on court at dusk → night court under floodlights. On-screen type beats: "Long lunch." / "Late match." / "A difficult habit to break." Ends on wordmark + "Racquet Habit — Est. 2026." *Build: Seedance 2.0 multi-shot from P-stills.*

**V3 — Collection-open film (15s).** Five locker doors open one by one, one per capsule; quick macro hits use only verified current details (embroidery, print, vessel art, case surface); end card "24 pieces. Five capsules. The collection is open." *Build: Seedance i2v from P8 + current product stills.*

**V5 — Tennis Lunch Tennis day arc (30s).** Three-chapter mini-doc: morning rally / the lunch (real time slows down, ice bucket, laughter) / afternoon rally. Chapter cards in Anton caps: COURT. LUNCH. COURT.

**V13 — Clubhouse ASMR (30–45s).** Macro + sound design: ice cracking over tennis balls, ball fuzz brushed, embroidery under fingertip, cotton snap, tumbler lid click, ball bounce echo. No voiceover, minimal type. Build the object-level ASMR now; add an unboxing version only after a physical sample proves the real packaging.

**V14 — Night court transition (15s).** Day outfit → ball toss at sunset → cut on impact → night court, floodlights, Night Court Reverse tee. Classic transition format, brand-flavored.

**V16 — "It started at love" (15–20s).** A cherry-red ball-flight line travels across real scenes — court, lunch table, Love Cherries MagSafe case — and resolves beside a current Love Cherries tee without redrawing its print. Type: "Every scoreline starts at love."

## §2 Founder & story (trust layer)

**V2 — Founder intro (30–45s, talking head or VO).** "I started a tennis brand in Yerevan…" — why: the hours around play deserved better objects. Show sketches, print files, first samples. Honest, specific, zero hype. *This style of video is consistently the highest-converting content class for new DTC brands.*

**V15 — The long lunch vlog (45s).** Semi-real: set the baseline table from P1, narrate club rules deadpan ("Rule 3: the ice bucket is not optional").

**V19 — Comment-reply videos (ongoing, 15–30s).** Reply to real comments ("is the racquet included?" — "the racquet is emotional support only"). Cheapest trust content that exists; TikTok's reply-with-video feature.

## §3 UGC-style (performance layer — these become the ads)

**V7 — Real sample unboxing (20–30s; blocked until sample arrives).** Film the actual Fourthwall shipment exactly as received, because the outer mailer and packing method vary by fulfillment partner and are not brand-controlled. Do not generate branded tissue, boxes, or mailers. If the optional Fourthwall custom insert is enabled, show the real 6×4 or 4×6 card. *Marketing Studio Unboxing can be used only as a shot-planning proxy, never as packaging evidence.*

**V10 — GRWM: court to lunch (30–45s).** Creator dresses in one current tee/crop, the Out of Office Court Cap, and Something Cold tote; ends at café table with racquet. VO covers fit honestly only after samples or creator products are in hand.

**V25 — Try-on haul (30–60s).** One creator, 3–4 current pieces, quick cuts. AI virtual try-on copy stays visual ("crop / oversized / night") and avoids material or fit testimony until real samples validate it. *Marketing Studio: UGC Virtual Try-On.*

**V26 — Selfie testimonial (15–20s).** Front-camera energy: "I bought the Emotional Support Racquet tee as a joke. It's now my personality." *Marketing Studio: Selfie Testimonial mode.*

**V11 — What's in my court bag (30s).** Bag dump: racquet, balls, Something Cold tote, Out of Office cap, Serve Chilled tumbler, tee backup, cherries. Every item gets a one-liner.

**V27 — "TikTok made me buy it" (20s).** Package → try-on → verdict, standard high-performing arc with the brand's dry twist: "10/10 would form a habit."

## §4 Humor & series (reach layer)

**V4 — POV meme (10–15s).** "POV: you said 'last one' three sets ago." Static-ish scene, big type, trending audio.
**V6 — "5 signs you have a racquet habit" (20–30s).** List video; sign #5 is always "you own the shirt."
**V8 — Collection tease (10s).** Locker cracked open, silhouettes only, "Tomorrow."
**V9 — Rating members' rest days (30s).** Photo-review format: lounger-by-the-court 9/10, "watching doubles from a café" 8/10, actual rest 2/10.
**V12 — Court-side hydration (15s).** Match-score narration follows a pour into the Court-Side Hydration travel mug, then a Serve Chilled tumbler; the pause before "one more set" does the joke.
**V17 — Gift guide (20s).** "Gifts for the person whose weekend is a set score" — caps/bottles fast cuts, prices on screen.
**V18 — Something Cold travel meme (15s).** Packing for a "relaxing" trip: racquet goes in first, Something Cold tote second. Weekender b-roll.
**V20–V24 — Open-season pack.** Hype without trademarks (V20), "what to wear to a night match" (V21), watch-party kit (V22), "tennis whites, loosely interpreted" (V23), spectator POV during a tiebreak (V24).

## §6 Creator/UGC program

- **Wave 1 (launch):** 5–10 micro-creators (1–20K; tennis players, tennis-girl-aesthetic, pickleball). Offer: 1–2 current-catalog pieces + $50–150 per deliverable + usage rights incl. Spark/whitelisting for 90 days. Brief: shoot V7/V10/V25 style after receipt, natural light, honest voice; give the hook bank, not a script to read.
- **Wave 2 (month 2):** double down on whoever converted; add 1–2 pickleball/padel creators for cross-sport reach; open a standing "members archive" hashtag (#racquethabit) reposted weekly (C31).
- **Rights hygiene:** written usage terms (organic + paid, platforms, duration); disclose partnerships per platform rules (#ad); keep AI-generated "UGC" and real creator UGC clearly separated in ad-account labeling — AI ones get the platform's AI toggles (details in file 07 §8).
