## Acceptance criteria

- Original rather than a collage of tennis conventions.
- Coherent with New Court Classic across the entire system.
- Appropriate for the final material and scale.
- No malformed equipment, hands, seams, handles, or typography.
- No third-party marks or near-copies.
- Vector candidates can be faithfully reconstructed with clean paths.
- Raster imagery supports the website crop without hiding the subject or fighting interface copy.
