# Racquet Habit — ChatGPT project context

## Purpose

This project is the working identity studio for Racquet Habit, a contemporary tennis fashion, objects, and hospitality brand based in Yerevan.

The task is not to imitate a historic tennis brand or make generic tennis merchandise. Develop an original, recognizable identity system that can live across garments, embroidery, drinkware, packaging, editorial campaigns, and a premium ecommerce experience.

## Brand platform

- Name: **RACQUET HABIT**
- Descriptor: **TENNIS ADDICTS SOCIETY**
- Origin line: **YEREVAN · EST. 2026**
- Primary line: **A DIFFICULT HABIT TO BREAK.**
- Supporting line: **ONE MORE SET.**
- Positioning: the unofficial society for people who always have time for one more set.
- Collection 01: **THE LAST SET**

## Creative territory — New Court Classic

Combine:

- contemporary European tournament elegance;
- refined sporting utility;
- fashion-editorial scale and restraint;
- tactile screen print, embroidery, woven labels, cotton, canvas, and uncoated paper;
- sunlit court and quiet clubhouse culture;
- dry humour used as small-print wit.

The identity should feel cultivated, energetic, human, and physically credible. It may nod to championship culture through colour, proportion, court geometry, sporting stripes, and typographic discipline, but it must never copy Wimbledon, Lacoste, Wilson, Roland-Garros, or another supplied reference.

## Character

- elegant, not luxurious for luxury's sake;
- sporting, not performance-tech;
- established in taste, not fake-aged;
- expressive, not decorative;
- warm and social, not nocturnal or aloof;
- witty, not gimmicky.

## Palette

- Centre Court Green `#103C2C`
- Championship Ivory `#F3EBD8`
- Tournament Navy `#18283D`

- Service Red `#B83D34`
- Ball Yellow `#D7E63D`
- Chalk White `#FFFDF7`
- Scoreboard Ink `#1B1A17`

Ordinary identity applications should use green, ivory, and at most one accent. Red and yellow are interruptions, not broad fields.

## Typography direction

- Editorial display: high-contrast Roman serif with sporting poise rather than fragile fashion thinness.
- Supporting type: disciplined grotesk.
- Utility data: narrow/condensed sans.
- The word `Habit` requires bespoke lettering. It should feel fast, assured, and hand-authored—closer to a tournament-programme signature or confident brush italic than wedding calligraphy.

## Identity priorities

1. A distinctive primary wordmark with excellent one-colour reproduction.
2. An RH monogram with a strong silhouette at 12–15 mm embroidery size.
3. A horizontal Society seal/patch that works at 65 mm woven-patch size and 250 mm print size.
4. A restrained supporting graphic system: championship stripe, ball-seam gesture, court geometry, and court-object line illustrations.
5. Campaign and product imagery that feels real enough to direct an owned photoshoot later.

## Production constraints

- Evaluate identity concepts in black and white before colour.
- Marks must work flat, without gradients, shadows, chrome, 3D, or mockup lighting.
- Avoid hairlines and detail that disappears in embroidery or screen print.
- Do not approve lettering solely from generated text. Treat generations as concept references, then redraw and optically correct as vectors.
- Use no more than four spot colours in a production graphic and usually one or two.
- Preserve generous clear space and strong negative space.

## Hard exclusions

No crowns, laurels, heraldic shields, crossed-racquet crests, tennis-ball letter dots, fake founding dates, distressed faux-vintage texture, generic country-club badges, ultra-thin tech monograms, black-on-black luxury mood, neon/cyber aesthetics, gradients, chrome, glassmorphism, wedding calligraphy, clip art, third-party logos, or nonsense brand text.

## Prompt and review protocol

For every asset thread:

1. Restate the asset's functional job and production constraints.
2. Generate three meaningfully different directions—not superficial colour swaps.
3. Present concepts on a flat neutral ground with no mockup unless the thread is specifically for mockups.
4. Critique silhouette, originality, hierarchy, legibility, reproducibility, and relationship to the complete system.
5. Select one direction or describe one targeted adjustment before regenerating.
6. Keep exact requested text verbatim and add no extra copy.

Browser submission safety for this project:

- Insert follow-up prompts as a single uninterrupted line; ChatGPT's rich-text
  composer can submit at paragraph breaks during automated multiline entry.
- Before sending, verify both the exact opening clause and exact final clause
  are present in the composer value.
- After sending, verify the message appears in full and generation has begun.
- Never reuse or modify another open ChatGPT project tab.

The current files are working prototypes, not untouchable masters. Improve them when a stronger, more ownable solution emerges.
